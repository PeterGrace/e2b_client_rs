## Follow-ups (M4 candidates, not filed)

- A zero timeout means a 1 ms deadline, consistently across
  `StreamInputOptions`, `PtyOptions` and `WatchOptions`; M4's API review
  should decide whether zero should mean "no deadline", as in the SDK.
- `write_files` with octet-stream sends every upload at once, with no
  concurrency limit (documented; a fail-fast test exists).
- `Pty::connect` takes a raw `Option<Duration>` rather than an options struct.
- A PTY's deadline kills the shell, and the session can't be reattached
  (documented).
- `Pty::connect` to a pid that has exited returned `Ok` during Task 3; not
  investigated.
- `is_symlink` (#30).
- A full sandbawks run needs cluster headroom and reruns 120 s apart, so the
  two-sandbox warm pool can refill.
